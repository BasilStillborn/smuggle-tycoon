Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

UK Arrival Kit is an independent travel utility for visitors arriving in Britain. It is not an official UK government service.

Information We Collect

The app can collect the arrival profile you choose in the app, such as country, language, arrival airport, destination city, trip type, and trip length. This is used to personalise the app experience.

If you join the validation list, the app sends your email address and selected arrival context to our configured form provider, currently Formspree.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which tools are useful, including tile opens, app-link clicks, feedback selections, currency tool usage, and waitlist submissions. We do not intentionally send your quick translation text to analytics.

Quick Translation

The quick translation text you type is not stored by this app. If you choose Google Translate or Baidu Translate, your text is sent to that external service in order to translate it. Their own privacy policies apply.

External Services

The app links to external services such as Google Translate, Baidu Translate, TfL, Citymapper, National Rail, Deliveroo, Uber Eats, Just Eat, Uber, Bolt, Met Office, and other practical travel tools. These third parties may collect data according to their own policies.

Currency Rates

The GBP/CNY converter uses a third-party exchange-rate endpoint and caches the latest rate locally where possible. Exchange rates are for information only and may differ from your bank, card issuer, or payment platform.

Local Storage

The app may use browser or WebView local storage to remember your arrival profile and cached currency rate on your device.

Contact and Support

For support, use the app feedback/waitlist form or visit the Support page.
