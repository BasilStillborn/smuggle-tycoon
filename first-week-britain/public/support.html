Support

UK Arrival Kit helps Chinese visitors prepare for the first week in Britain with translation, payment, transport, delivery, taxi, currency, and emergency tools.

For product feedback or support, open the app and use the feedback/waitlist form in the "加入名单" window.

Common Issues

External app links do not open: update your browser or app version, then try again with a stable internet connection.

Currency rate is unavailable: the app uses a cached or fallback GBP/CNY rate. It is only an estimate.

Translation opens another service: Google Translate and Baidu Translate are external services. UK Arrival Kit does not store your typed translation text.

Important

This app is an independent guide. It is not an emergency service, legal adviser, financial adviser, medical provider, payment provider, taxi operator, delivery service, airline, airport, or official UK government service.

In an emergency in the UK, call 999.
